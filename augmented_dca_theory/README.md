# Augmented DCA Theory

Mathematical derivations, proofs, and early simulations for the timing-augmented Dollar-Cost Averaging strategy.

### Overview
- **Capital Invariance Proof:** Mathematical demonstration showing that timing alpha ($\eta$) is strictly independent of the invested amount ($M$).
- **Volatility Capture Axiom:** Demonstrating that outperformance relies entirely on price oscillation density and is diluted over time by accumulated portfolio size[cite: 1].
- **Reference:** See Section 2 of [`final_report(in french).pdf`] for full proofs and derivations.
